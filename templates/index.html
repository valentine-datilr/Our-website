Sunlight Exposure

Sunlight exposure corresponds to how much sunlight your room is getting

  • Full sunlight (direct rays of sun during multiple hours a day), usually a south-facing window
  • Medium (bright indirect light) = ideal for most plants, close to a sun-exposed window but not in direct sunlight, usually an east- or west-facing window, no direct light around noon
  • Low light (also called semi-shade) = somewhere further away from a window, no direct sunlight during the day

Humidity Level

  • Humidity in most homes is around 30%, you can use a humidifier or spray water multiple times a day around the plant to increase humidity. Alternatively, if you have a well lit bathroom or kitchen, you can put plants there!
